Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 20, 2026

At DENTIX, we are committed to protecting your privacy and your patients' data.

1. Data Collection

We collect necessary data to provide our services, including:

First: Clinic Data

  • Clinic Name
  • Logo
  • Contact Info

Second: User Data

  • Name
  • Email
  • Login Credentials
  • Role

Third: Patient Data

  • Name
  • Medical Records
  • Medical Attachments
  • Financial Data

2. Legal Basis

We process data based on:

  • User Consent (Clinic)
  • Contract Performance
  • Regulatory Compliance

3. Data Security

We implement strict technical measures, including:

  • Encryption at Rest
  • Encryption in Transit (HTTPS)
  • Multi-Tenant Isolation
  • Access Controls

4. Data Usage

Data is used for:

  • System Operation
  • Performance Improvement
  • Operational Notifications
Important: We do not share or sell patient data to third parties for marketing.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We may use technical service providers (like hosting) who strictly adhere to data protection.

6. Data Retention

We retain data for the duration of the subscription.

7. User Rights

As a clinic owner/manager, you have the right to:

  • Access your data anytime
  • Export data
  • Request permanent deletion

8. Breach Notification

In case of a security breach, affected clinics will be notified immediately.

9. Cookies

We use cookies solely for operational purposes:

  • Session Management
  • User Preferences

We do not use advertising tracking cookies.

10. Policy Amendments

We reserve the right to amend this policy when necessary.

11. Contact

For privacy inquiries, please contact us via official channels.
