How Subscription Activation Works

To ensure smooth onboarding tailored to each clinic's specific practice setup, subscriptions are coordinated directly with our system administration and support team via WhatsApp or official email.

Medical Data Guarantee: Your patient health records, clinical notes, and treatment histories remain permanently accessible in read-only mode even if your active subscription term expires, safeguarding continuous clinical patient care.

Billing and Inquiries

Plans are billed monthly in Egyptian Pounds (EGP). To activate your chosen plan or discuss your practice requirements, select any plan card button above or message our team directly via WhatsApp at +201010285288.
