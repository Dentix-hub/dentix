Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Dentix and how it helps you manage your dental clinic.

How do I start using Dentix for my clinic?
You can begin immediately by clicking "Create Clinic Account", filling in your clinic details, and setting up your login credentials to start using the system directly in your browser.
Does Dentix require installing special software on clinic devices?
No. Dentix requires no local installation or on-premise servers. It is a cloud platform accessible through any modern web browser on authorized computers and tablets.
Does the system support both Arabic and English?
Yes, Dentix provides a fully bilingual interface in Arabic and English, allowing doctors and staff members to work in their preferred language.
What core features are included in Dentix?
Dentix includes digital patient records, appointment scheduling with waiting queue management, clinical dental charting, and payment tracking with clinic financial reports.
