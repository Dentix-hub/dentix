Dental Appointments & Clinic Financial Management

Organizing daily schedules, managing patient queues, collecting balances, and monitoring laboratory costs.

Practice success depends on clinical excellence paired with reliable operational efficiency and clear financial tracking. Dentix provides comprehensive tools to manage daily bookings, recall workflows, patient invoices, and clinic expense ledgers.

Streamlined Appointment Books & Daily Waiting Queues

Dentix appointment scheduling reduces patient wait times and helps coordinate appointments between the reception desk and dentists:

  • Live Appointment Statuses: Track each visit status in real time (Scheduled, Confirmed, Arrived/Waiting, In Chair, Completed, Cancelled).
  • Front-Desk Waiting Queue: Direct visibility of seated patients to maintain orderly, timely patient transitions.
  • Recall & Multi-Visit Follow-Up Queues: Review follow-up requests and schedule the next visit for treatments that need more than one session.

Comprehensive Practice Financial Controls

Dental finances require specialized accounting to handle multi-stage clinical treatments, external lab fees, and consumable material costs:

  • Procedure Invoicing & Payment Receipts: Immediate generation of itemized receipts detailing treatments rendered and amounts received.
  • Outstanding Patient Balances: Accurate ledger tracking of remaining patient debt, facilitating prompt collection upon each visit.
  • Dental Lab Order & Cost Reconciliation: Track laboratory orders (crowns, bridges, prosthetics) and log technician dues to prevent financial discrepancies.
  • Clinic Operational Overhead Logging: Document daily expenses (disposables, sterilization materials, rent, utilities) for transparent net margin calculations.

Actionable Reporting for Practice Growth

The platform compiles regular management summaries, including:

  • Total revenue collections categorized by payment method (cash, card, bank transfer).
  • Net practice income after deducting clinic operating expenses and technician costs.
  • Daily appointment lists and the current status of each visit.
