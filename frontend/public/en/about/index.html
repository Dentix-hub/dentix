About Dentix — Dental Clinic Management Software

Comprehensive cloud platform designed specifically for dental practitioners, clinics, and multi-provider centers.

Dentix streamlines daily dental practice operations by bringing digital patient health records, interactive dental charting, appointment scheduling, and clinic financial management into one cohesive web platform.

What is Dentix?

Dentix is modern cloud-based dental practice management software created to help dentists and clinic staff manage administrative and clinical workflows seamlessly. The platform enables secure access from desktop computers and tablets without requiring on-premise local servers or complicated IT hardware.

Who is Dentix For?

  • Solo Dental Practices: To maintain digital patient records, monitor clinical treatments, and access treatment history at the operatory chair.
  • Dental Centers & Polyclinics: To organize appointment flows, coordinate receptionists with doctors, and manage operational clinic finances.
  • Receptionists & Practice Managers: To track the waiting room queue, schedule recall visits, collect payments, and issue clear treatment invoices.

Why Choose Cloud Dental Management with Dentix?

Traditional dental practices frequently encounter lost paper folders, double-booked appointments, uncollected patient balances, and untracked dental laboratory costs. Dentix solves these bottlenecks through:

  • Unified Digital Patient Records: Personal information, comprehensive medical alerts, past visit notes, and attached documents in one place.
  • Interactive Clinical Dental Chart (Odontogram): Visual tooth-by-tooth diagnosis and surface-level procedure documentation.
  • Flexible Appointment Management: Real-time status progression from scheduled to arrived, in-chair, and completed.
  • Financial Clarity: Transparent billing per procedure, payment receipts, discounts, remaining patient balances, and clinic expense logging.

Bilingual Architecture (Arabic & English)

Dentix natively supports both Arabic and English with full bidirectional typography (RTL/LTR). Each staff member can configure their preferred interface language independently, fostering effective collaboration across clinical and administrative personnel.

Typical Daily Practice Workflow

  1. Check-in & Reception: Register new patients or retrieve existing files, confirm appointments, and move patients to the waiting queue.
  2. Clinical Examination: The dentist reviews the dental chart, examines surfaces, and establishes a treatment plan.
  3. Care Session Documentation: Record procedure progress, endodontic canal measurements, materials, and clinical observations.
  4. Billing & Recall: Settle payment receipts, update outstanding ledger balances, and schedule the next follow-up visit.
